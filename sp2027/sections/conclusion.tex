\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
A ranked election can be verifiable without publishing its ballots or
its pairwise counts, using only $\Sigma$-protocols. Anchoring the pairwise bits
to a position vector makes ballot validity a conjunction of proofs, and
certifying the strongest-path matrix as a closure turns the Schulze
winner into $O(m^3)$ proofs over committed values. The only cleartext
besides the winner is the witness paths, which reveal some defeat
directions and no margin. Because the library already proves results
about each step, ballot and certificate soundness are machine-checked on
top of it, and the extracted code certified Gentoo's winner in all
thirteen council elections. What remains before a deployment is wiring
(threshold decryption, the voter's identity in the Fiat--Shamir
challenge, and a decoder that enforces the group), which is where the
Belenios defects of Section~\ref{sec:eval:oracle} were. Batching the
certificate as we batched the ballot, and certifying the closure without
witness paths, are the next steps.
