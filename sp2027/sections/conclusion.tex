\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
A ranked election can be verifiable without publishing its ballots or
its pairwise counts, using only $\Sigma$-protocols. Anchoring the
pairwise bits to a position vector lets each ballot be checked on its
own, with membership proofs. Proving that the
strongest-path matrix is a closure turns the Schulze winner into
$O(m^3)$ proofs over encrypted values. The only cleartext besides the
winner is the witness paths, which reveal the direction of some defeats
and no margin. Every proof comes from the certified library, so ballot
and certificate soundness are machine-checked on top of it, and the
extracted code certified Gentoo's winner in all thirteen council
elections. The next steps are to batch the certificate as we batched the
ballot, and to certify the closure without witness paths.
