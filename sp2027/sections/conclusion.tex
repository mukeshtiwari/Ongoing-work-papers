\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
A ranked election can be verifiable without publishing its ballots or
its pairwise counts, using only $\Sigma$-protocols. Anchoring the
pairwise bits to a position vector lets each ballot be checked on its
own, with membership proofs. Proving that the
strongest-path matrix is a closure turns the Schulze winner into
$O(m^3)$ proofs over encrypted values, of size linear in the electorate.
The only cleartext besides the winner is the witness paths, which reveal
the direction of some defeats and no margin. Every proof comes from the certified library, so ballot
and certificate soundness are machine-checked on top of it, and the
extracted code certified the first-seat winner of all thirteen Gentoo
council elections. The next steps are to make the certificate
logarithmic in the electorate, to certify the closure without witness
paths, and to mechanise the Fiat--Shamir step.
